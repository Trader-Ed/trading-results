## Method

Calendar: T16's same-day-expiration days (one bit per QuantConnect trading day). Sessions come from
data/bench/sessions.csv (from September 2015) and, before that, weekdays minus the NYSE holidays listed below
(the counts match T16's trading days for 2012-2015). Each window covers at most 55 expiry days of one year so
that its log stays under the free plan's 10 KB.
Bench check (step 0): on one fixed day the algorithm logs the four legs' quotes at entry and exit and every fill
for one iron condor (IC20-1000-E1). The P&L is calculated by hand from those quotes (sells at the bid, buys at
the ask, $0.05 per contract per leg per side) and must equal the backtest's P&L to the cent; every fill must be
at the bid (sells) or the ask (buys); and a second run on the same day filled at the mid must exceed the bid/ask
run by exactly the sum of the half-spreads of the eight fills.
Pass bar per variant, SPY only, 2012-2023: average P&L per trade after costs at least 3 standard errors above
zero (one trade per day, so the plain standard error is already clustered by day); positive in at least 9 of
the 12 years, counting only years with at least 30 trades; largest peak-to-trough drop no worse than $625
(one contract); positive over 2018, 2020 and 2022 together.

Algorithm: real orders through a custom fill model (every sell at the minute's bid, every buy at the ask, per leg) and a fee model of $0.05 per contract per leg per side; the P&L comes from the fills. Short strikes: the contract nearest the target |delta| among same-day contracts with a two-sided quote at entry, using QuantConnect's default option price model (IndicatorBasedOptionPriceModel: implied volatility from the mid quote).
