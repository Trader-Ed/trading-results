## Method

Version 2 rules (rulebook.yaml, partner approval 2026-11-18) on every 2026 session, all six names (AAPL, AMZN, TSLA, NVDA, QQQ, SPY), each on its own: the warm-up from the previous session's last 90 regular bars; squeeze entries from the 9:44 bar, pullback and failed bounce from the 9:59 bar, the last from the 11:49 bar; no new entries for 30 minutes after a 10:00 release; one trade at a time per name and a 5-minute wait after a stop-out; the volume step removed. Contracts: at the money or the first strike in the money (pullback, failed bounce), the first strike out of the money (squeeze); same-day expiry, else the nearest. Entry at the open of the contract's first 1-minute bar after the signal bar closes; the trailing stop on the stock (the larger of $0.25 and 1.5 x the 1-minute ATR, from the stock's open in the entry minute) checked inside each stock bar worst case first; exit at the option's close in the stop's minute, or the 12:30 open.
Two profit figures: option trade prices minus the $2.00 commission; and bought at the estimated ask, sold at the estimated bid, minus the same. The spread estimate is the median relative spread of the option quotes the engine logged in November 2026, by name and price band (few quotes: it is an estimate, not a measurement).
Limits: Alpaca keeps no historical option quotes, so a limit order that would have been missed can't be known and every signal counts as filled; the stop is checked on 1-minute bars, not every second; there is no account-wide cash limit; earnings days are not tagged; option bars are trades, and a contract with no trade in a minute takes its last earlier trade.
